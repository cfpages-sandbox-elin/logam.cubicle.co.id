# Topical Authority — logam.cubicle.co.id

## Role and boundary

Despite its hostname, the current repository presents a brass-and-copper craft/catalog business rather than metal cubicle systems. The knowledge plan follows the observed entity: decorative, functional, lighting, furniture, sanitary, and architectural objects made from copper/brass. Transactional catalog routes own availability, quotation, customization, shipping, and ordering. Exact alloy, structural, electrical, plumbing, food-contact, heritage, environmental, origin, and performance claims require product-specific evidence and qualified review.

## Context used

- Homepage: “Jual Kerajinan Kuningan & Tembaga.”
- Repository: 1,052 tracked files.
- Sitemap evidence: three XML files with 1,077 `<loc>` entries.
- Meaningful route families: bath tub, bowl, calligraphy, lamps, copper tile, decoration, furniture/table, glass/mirror frame, guci/vase, kubah masjid, relief/statue, sinks, wall clock, and water fountain.

## Ignored template noise

The 489 root-level location-swapped sales pages, regional pages, categories/feeds/comments, duplicated spelling routes, generic blog/sample surfaces, repeated metadata, and WordPress assets were counted but excluded from topic decisions. The hostname/content mismatch is a deployment and branding evidence gate; it does not justify inventing cubicle coverage that the source does not support.
